# My Contributions — NTUA Llama 500M

## Project Overview

This project involved the development and pretraining of a 500-million-parameter Llama-based language model as part of a collaborative university initiative at the National Technical University of Athens (NTUA).

The model was trained using **Nanotron**, a distributed training framework for large language models.

My contributions focused primarily on the **pretraining pipeline**, including training framework debugging, model architecture configuration, dataset selection, and pretraining curriculum design.

## My Technical Contributions

### 1. Nanotron Debugging and Training Configuration

- Investigated and debugged issues encountered while working with the Nanotron training framework.
- Troubleshot problems affecting the pretraining workflow and its configuration.
- Contributed to the preparation and adjustment of training configurations.

### 2. Model Architecture and Hyperparameter Selection

- Participated in selecting the architectural configuration of the approximately 500M-parameter language model.
- Worked on decisions involving model depth, width, and related architectural parameters.
- Considered architectural trade-offs within the target model size and available computational resources.

### 3. Pretraining Dataset Selection and Mixture Design

- Contributed to selecting the datasets used for model pretraining.
- Worked on determining the composition of the pretraining data mixture.
- Participated in defining the relative proportions of different data sources within the training corpus.

### 4. Pretraining Curriculum and Stage Planning

- Contributed to planning the progression of training data across different pretraining stages.
- Worked on determining which categories of content would be emphasized at each stage.
- Participated in organizing the pretraining strategy to guide the model's learning progression.

## Scope of Contribution

My work was concentrated on the **design, configuration, and debugging of the pretraining process**, rather than downstream model evaluation or qualitative testing.

The project provided practical experience in language model architecture design, pretraining data strategy, and troubleshooting distributed training workflows.
